Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Who uses this tool

StoryShorts is used only by the owner of ScrollTales, for ScrollTales' own accounts. It does not collect data from viewers or from anyone else.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (with the owner's consent): permission to upload videos, read the channel's own videos, and read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, likes, comments, shares, subscribers).
  • TikTok (with the owner's consent): basic account information (display name and account ID), permission to upload videos to the owner's account, and the account's own video list and statistics.

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted, and are never shown in logs or videos. Statistics are stored locally to show performance. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: in Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Disconnecting in the tool deletes the stored tokens.
