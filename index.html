ScrollTales

Short stories, told over Minecraft parkour.

ScrollTales publishes short vertical videos: original fictional stories narrated over gameplay footage recorded by the channel owner. Every story is written for entertainment and labelled as fiction, and the narration is AI-generated.

The videos are produced by StoryShorts, a private tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uses the official YouTube and TikTok APIs only to upload ScrollTales' own videos to ScrollTales' own accounts and to read their performance statistics. It is not offered to other users.
